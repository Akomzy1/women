## Inside the guide

### A clear place to start — and a reference to return to.

Know Your Cycle is a 28-page illustrated PDF covering:

- Your anatomy and the phases of the menstrual cycle.
- Counting cycle length and separating it from bleeding days.
- Keeping records that are useful in a care conversation.
- Common concerns, including painful, heavy or late periods.
- Plain-language introductions to menstrual-health terms and conditions.
- Period products and changes across different life stages.
- Reasons to seek medical help and ways to prepare for a visit.
- A practical next-step plan, glossary and linked sources.

The guide is educational. It helps readers understand the topics and prepare questions; it does not identify the cause of an individual's symptoms.
